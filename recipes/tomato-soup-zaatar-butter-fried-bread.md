# Tomato soup with za’atar and butter-fried skillet bread

## Ingredients

### Soup

- Campbell’s canned tomato soup
- Water as directed on the can, adjusted to taste
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Za’atar, to taste

### Bread

Suggested starting batch; these flour and water amounts were suggested, not confirmed as the amounts actually used:

- ½ cup flour
- Pinch of salt
- About 3 tablespoons water, adjusted to make a soft dough
- Butter, to taste; start small. Exact tested quantity was not recorded.

## Steps

1. Prepare and heat the soup according to the can’s instructions. Adjust the water to taste; the amount used on the first try was not recorded.
2. Season with salt, pepper, and za’atar. Taste and adjust; the za’atar amount was not recorded.
3. Mix the flour and pinch of salt. Add water gradually until a soft dough forms. Rest for 10 minutes.
4. Divide into two pieces and roll each about ⅛ inch thick.
5. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Add a small amount of butter, then the bread. Cook roughly 1–2 minutes per side, until cooked through with brown spots; adjust the heat as needed so the butter does not burn.
6. Serve the warm bread with the soup.

## Tried and liked — October 3, 2026

The za’atar from a friend added a liked herby spice to the tomato soup. Butter went into the pan first by accident, and the resulting butter-fried bread was a favorite variation. Keep that buttery fried texture next time.

No yeast, baking powder, or oven needed.
